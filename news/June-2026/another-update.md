# New Update (June 15, 2026)

Hi players! blueskychan_ here! We're rolling out some updates to osu!somtum to make the player experience better.

A week ago we re-opened osu!somtum, and we've been getting a lot of feedback and help. Thanks, everyone! Now let's get to the topics.

## 1. We now support donations via Stripe!

After experimenting with the Stripe gateway, we officially support donations through Stripe!

![viastripe](https://i.postimg.cc/PrfXLSV0/image.png)
![checkout](https://i.postimg.cc/BZpsGxZZ/image.png)

If Stripe won't accept your card, please try another card (this is usually a general Stripe issue) or try another option in Stripe. We may enable more options soon, but for now we only accept Mastercard and Visa.

Please note that abusing our payment gateway can lead to account restrictions.

## 2. Another anti-cheat update

Our current anti-cheat has some problems with osu!rx and other game modes, like in the example below.

![abuseac](https://i.postimg.cc/wMvrzRF2/image.png)

We're updating the anti-cheat to support as many game modes as possible, but remember that **the anti-cheat can make mistakes** — we still rely on community help! If your account gets falsely restricted by it, please contact us at accounts@somtum.fun or open a Discord ticket ASAP.

We'll do our best to make the anti-cheat as good as possible. We don't want to run a lot of checks on the client side due to privacy concerns.

## 3. We now support updating beatmap files! (osu! client upload only)

We've been working hard to figure out how osu!osz2 works. Previously, beatmap uploaders had to remove their beatmap from osu!somtum and re-upload it, which wasn't ideal. So we've made some backend changes that let you update a beatmap (.osu) without removing and re-uploading it — just like osu!

## 4. Beatmap page update

We've improved the beatmapsets page. Players can now edit their own beatmap descriptions, just like on osu!.

![beatmapsetsdesc](https://i.postimg.cc/kX3DYN5Y/image.png)

With beatmap discussion, you can now hype your favorite beatmaps, see nominations, and post problems, suggestions, and praise — just like osu!!

![beatmapdis](https://i.postimg.cc/dtwwT533/image.png)
![comment](https://i.postimg.cc/3wpc1Hfc/image.png)

You can also report a comment if something isn't right!

![reportbutton](https://i.postimg.cc/N01dTkLn/image.png)

And that's everything we're updating in osu!somtum today! Thanks for playing on our server! Don't forget to vote for us at https://osu-server-list.com/server/somtum!
